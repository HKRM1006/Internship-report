### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
FCAJ provided a friendly and open working environment. The office offered a quiet workspace and felt more disciplined compared to self-studying at home.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**
Mentors were regularly active and answered questions in a timely manner. The admin team supported procedures and documentation, making it convenient for me to work at the office smoothly.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
The project allowed me to experiment with services related to Artificial Intelligence, which perfectly aligns with my AI major. I had the opportunity to build an AI-powered web application instead of working solely on experimental models as at school

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
Developed knowledge and practical skills in using AWS services. Had the opportunity to engage with speakers during event sessions, thereby acquiring valuable experience and real-world insights.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**
The company culture is very positive: everyone respects one another and works with great professionalism.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**
Interns can flexibly arrange and register their office attendance schedules according to their personal availability.
